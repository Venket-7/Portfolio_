SaaS Churn &
Revenue-at-Risk

A churn count alone can hide where retention matters most. This project connects account, usage, support, and subscription data to show both churn patterns and the revenue at risk.

~500B2B accounts
25K+usage records
5K+subscriptions
  • Identified about 10 historical subscription records per account and built a current-subscription table to prevent roughly 9× MRR overstatement.
  • Developed 20+ SQL queries across churn drivers, revenue risk, feature usage, support experience, and customer lifecycle.
  • Built a four-page Power BI dashboard showing 22% overall churn, from 31% in DevTools to 16% in Cybersecurity.
  • Highlighted Cybersecurity’s $279K revenue at risk, reframing retention priorities around value at stake.
